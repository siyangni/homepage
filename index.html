Job market paper

Differential Selection by Self-Control

Self-Control Problems and Entry into the Criminal Justice System from Adolescence to Early Adulthood

Young people with more self-control problems are more likely to receive a first police caution, arrest, or court appearance, even after accounting for the delinquency they report and the police stops they have experienced. Among youth already in contact with the police, self-control problems, not delinquency, predict formal processing. Official records reflect how the justice system responds to low self-control, not only how much young people offend.
