Teaching

Teaching statistics as reasoning

Many students in an intermediate statistics course do not see themselves as quantitative people.

In my experience the main obstacle is rarely the mathematics. Courses often teach the software step by step but leave the reasoning behind a method unstated, so students learn which button produces a p-value before they learn what question it answers. I teach the reasoning first and introduce the software after.
